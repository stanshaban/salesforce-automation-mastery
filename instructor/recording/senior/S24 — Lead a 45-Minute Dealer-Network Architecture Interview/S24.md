## 02 · Treat ambiguity as part of the task

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Treat ambiguity as part of the task». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

The interviewer says: 'Northstar is growing to one hundred dealers. Design its booking platform.' One hundred dealers does not tell you the number of users, booking rate, data distribution, business hours, external dependencies, or access policy. Do not fill every gap with an elaborate architecture and then defend the assumptions as facts. Start with the user journey. Anna needs to request a test-drive, Irina needs a trustworthy confirmation, and Oleg needs visibility into unresolved work. Ask whether dealers share customers, who owns vehicle availability, whether booking must survive a DMS outage, and what 'confirmed' means. These questions can change the model more than choosing a queue or an API.

## 03 · Use time to produce a coherent first version

## 04 · Connect scale to mechanisms

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Connect scale to mechanisms». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Do not use the dealer count as a direct formula for Apex limits. Discuss transaction shape, expected request rate, record concentration, query selectivity, and asynchronous completion capacity. Identify a likely pressure point under your stated assumptions. For example, many appointments updating one shared vehicle summary can create contention; a broad Requested-status filter can become unselective as data changes. Show what would reveal the problem: user-journey latency, relevant transaction counters, lock failures, oldest pending age, or reconciliation mismatches. Then describe a proportional response. Partition work by the actual shared resource, narrow a query without changing the requirement, or reduce unnecessary work before adding concurrency.

## 05 · Handle a changed requirement without defending sunk work

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Handle a changed requirement without defending sunk work». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Halfway through, the interviewer removes permission to keep a Pending state. Immediate authoritative confirmation is now required. Revisit the synchronous dependency, timeout behavior, and user experience. Do not insist that the original asynchronous design still satisfies a requirement it no longer meets. Explain what remains reusable: stable business identities, access checks, reservation idempotency, reconciliation, and observability. Explain what changes: the critical path, availability tradeoff, and permitted response during an outage. A revised decision demonstrates understanding more clearly than adding an unsupported guarantee.
