## 03 · Compare two plausible record models

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Compare two plausible record models». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

In one model, appointments are owned by individual salespeople, with a compact managerial role structure and dealer-specific sharing groups. In another, ownership is more centralized and access is assigned through explicit business groups. Neither is universally correct. Individual ownership supports some workflows naturally but makes staff turnover and transfers important. Central ownership can simplify one process while concentrating records and making sharing maintenance expensive. Role hierarchy access normally opens records upward through the hierarchy; two peers do not automatically gain each other's records just because their job titles match. Custom objects can have relevant hierarchy settings, so record the actual configuration. Public groups collect users or supported membership types for access rules; their maintenance and nesting also need operational ownership.

Test a transfer: Anna moves from East to West. Decide whether her historical East appointments remain visible, move to another owner, or follow a separate historical policy. Removing her from a group may not close access if she still owns the records or another sharing path remains.
