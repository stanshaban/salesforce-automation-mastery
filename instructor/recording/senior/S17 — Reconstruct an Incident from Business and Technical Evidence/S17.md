## 04 · Separate recovery from causal explanation

The immediate recovery task is to reconcile N-77 with the provider or apply the approved idempotent retry procedure. Blindly sending a new notification with a new operation key can create duplicates. The incident explanation is a separate task: why did an uncertain outcome disappear, and why did the dashboard misreport it? A cause statement should connect a mechanism to evidence. 'The developer was careless' explains neither the status transition nor the missing recovery state. A stronger statement identifies that code used the queuing acknowledgment as a completed-notification signal and did not persist timeout handling. Contributing conditions might include a test suite that covered only successful responses.

Add counts and age distributions for Pending notifications, attempts, confirmed provider acknowledgments, and unresolved outcomes. Alerting thresholds should reflect the business tolerance and observed normal behavior. Do not invent a universal threshold from this fictional incident. For longer interruptions, remember that an event bus is not an unlimited archive. Standard Platform Event and CDC retention is limited, and recovery beyond the available replay window may require reconciliation from durable business state. A checkpoint is useful, but it does not replace a recovery source.
