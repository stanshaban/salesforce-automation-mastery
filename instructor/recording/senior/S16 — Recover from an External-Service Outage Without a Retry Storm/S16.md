The scheduling service becomes unavailable while Anna and Max continue creating permitted Pending requests. If every failure retries immediately, the system produces more requests precisely when the dependency has less capacity. This is a retry storm: repeated attempts amplify the original outage and can consume Salesforce resources as well. Begin with failure classification. A malformed request missing a required vehicle key will not repair itself through repeated identical calls. A temporary connectivity failure might recover. A timeout is especially important because the remote system may have completed the operation before the response was lost. That attempt needs idempotency or reconciliation, not an assumption that nothing happened.

## 03 · Define a bounded policy

Backoff increases the delay between attempts. Jitter adds a controlled random spread so many workers do not retry at exactly the same instant. A retry bound limits the number of attempts or the total age of retryable work. These are policy choices to test against the service contract, not universal magic numbers. The synthetic policy below separates temporary errors from validation failures and unknown outcomes. Its delays are example design values, not Salesforce scheduling guarantees. A worker should store a future eligibility time or use an appropriate supported scheduling mechanism; do not hold an Apex transaction open by busy-waiting for the delay.

## 05 · Recover at a controlled rate

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Recover at a controlled rate». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

After the dependency returns, do not release the entire backlog at once. First verify a small probe, then increase the allowed rate while watching errors, response time, and queue age. Capacity must handle both new requests and old work. If arrivals equal completions, the backlog does not shrink. Use a tabletop model: 600 waiting items, 100 new items per minute, and a hypothetical recovered capacity of 120 completions per minute. The net drain is twenty per minute, so the model needs thirty minutes to clear the initial backlog, assuming those rates remain constant. This arithmetic is useful planning, not evidence of actual Salesforce or provider capacity.
