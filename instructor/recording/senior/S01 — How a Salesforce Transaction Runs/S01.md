Anna is a salesperson at Northstar Motors. She selects a Vehicle__c record and creates a Test_Drive__c for client Irina. The field definitions, validation rules, Apex classes, and Flow definitions are metadata: instructions that describe how the application behaves. The particular vehicle and appointment are business data. Changing a field definition and inserting an appointment are different operations, even though both can begin in Setup or a browser. A runtime is the software that interprets those definitions and executes the application. You do not need to know the physical database layout to design this feature. You do need to know which operations share a transaction. A transaction is a unit of work whose database changes can commit together or roll back together when its execution fails. The user's click is an entry point, not a guarantee that everything it eventually causes belongs to one transaction.

## 04 · Why shared infrastructure affects your design

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Why shared infrastructure affects your design». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Multitenancy means a platform serves multiple tenants while maintaining separation between their data and operations. An org is a tenant boundary; it is not permission for every user inside that org to see every record. Governor limits constrain individual executions, while other allocations apply across the org or a time window. These controls are part of the platform contract, so a design should budget work rather than assume unlimited processing. Hyperforce concerns Salesforce's infrastructure architecture and deployment on public-cloud infrastructure. It does not turn an inefficient nested loop into an efficient algorithm, merge independent transactions, or remove the application security model. Regional availability and organizational requirements can affect an infrastructure decision, but they do not establish a particular CPU entitlement for your code.
