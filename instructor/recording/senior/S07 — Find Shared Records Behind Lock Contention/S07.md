Record locking protects data consistency while an operation changes records. Contention occurs when concurrent operations need the same protected resource. Max and Anna may edit different appointments on screen yet trigger automation that updates the same Vehicle__c summary. Their visible records differ, but their transactions compete for a shared vehicle record. Start from the complete operation, including triggers, Flows, roll-ups, sharing maintenance, and relationship behavior. A lookup does not mean that every possible update always locks every ancestor. Locking depends on the object, relationship settings, and operations performed. The correct question is which shared record or membership resource this particular transaction needs, not whether two users clicked different rows.

## 03 · Recognize concentration in the data model

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Recognize concentration in the data model». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Data skew is an uneven distribution that concentrates work or access maintenance. Account skew can involve many children under one Account. Lookup skew concentrates references to one target. Ownership skew concentrates many records under one owner, potentially making role or sharing changes expensive. These patterns are warning signs, not automatic proof of the same failure mechanism. Northstar's synthetic model uses one 'Unassigned vehicle' record for many appointments. A handler also updates its Last_Activity__c on every appointment save. Last_Activity__c is a proposed exercise field. The model creates a hot record: a record many transactions repeatedly need. Replacing Anna's trigger with Queueable does not remove the shared target. It may simply move the contention into another queue.

## 04 · Choose a remedy that changes the cause

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Choose a remedy that changes the cause». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Partitioning groups work so concurrent workers operate on independent resources. For this example, route appointments for the same vehicle through one coordinated stream while allowing different vehicles to proceed independently. Merely sorting a file is not enough if the ingestion mechanism later splits it into overlapping parallel work. Verify the actual partition boundary. Serialization processes conflicting work one at a time. It can improve useful throughput when parallel attempts spend most of their time failing and retrying, although it reduces theoretical concurrency. Shortening the transaction can reduce how long a lock is held. Removing an unnecessary summary write may be better than optimizing its retry policy.

## 05 · Use a trace or a small controlled experiment

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Use a trace or a small controlled experiment». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

A small training org may not reproduce a contention error reliably. Scheduling and timing are not under your complete control, so a demonstration that happened not to fail does not disprove the mechanism. You can still inspect a proposed automation graph, count shared targets, and compare the intended partitioning. If you run a small experiment, use synthetic records, a bounded number of operations, and an explicit stop condition. Record completed operations, failures, retry count, and elapsed wall time. Do not add artificial busy loops or unbounded traffic merely to force a lock. The goal is to evaluate a hypothesis safely, not to stress a shared learning environment.
