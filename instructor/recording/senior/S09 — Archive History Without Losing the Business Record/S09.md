## 02 · Older does not automatically mean unnecessary

Anna usually needs upcoming appointments and recent follow-ups. Oleg also needs historical conversion reports, while Irina may ask about a past visit. These journeys have different access patterns. Archiving moves or manages information so it no longer burdens the main operational path while remaining available under an explicit retrieval policy. Deletion removes it from that path and may eventually make recovery impossible. A backup supports recovery after loss or corruption. An archive supports deliberate long-term retention and retrieval. Audit history explains changes or events, and is not necessarily a complete copy of every business record and relationship. One mechanism may help with more than one purpose, but you must verify its actual guarantees rather than treating the labels as synonyms.

Keeping history in core Salesforce preserves familiar relationships and reporting, but it may maintain storage and access-maintenance costs.
