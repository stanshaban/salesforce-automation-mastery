## 02 · A decision record preserves the reason

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «A decision record preserves the reason». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

An architecture decision record, or ADR, captures a significant choice and why it was made. It should name the problem, relevant constraints, considered alternatives, selected approach, and consequences. Its value is not the number of headings. A future developer should understand which assumption would justify revisiting the choice. Northstar must decide whether booking confirmation waits for the scheduling service or first creates a Pending request. The answer depends on the product promise, acceptable delay, outage behavior, and duplicate-prevention contract. 'Async is more scalable' is too broad to settle it. It hides the important question of whether Anna may tell Irina the slot is confirmed.

## 03 · Compare alternatives under the same conditions

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Compare alternatives under the same conditions». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Consider synchronous reservation, asynchronous reservation with a Pending state, and a hybrid that performs a fast check but confirms later. Evaluate each against the same requirements. A hybrid check does not reserve a slot unless the provider contract explicitly says it does; a result can become stale before confirmation. Record uncertainty honestly. If provider response-time evidence is unavailable, mark it unknown and propose a test. If the business has not accepted a Pending state, treat that as a decision dependency, not as permission to implement it silently.

## 05 · Make review a collaborative verification process

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Make review a collaborative verification process». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Separate blocking correctness or security issues, non-blocking maintainability improvements, and questions requiring product clarification. Ask for evidence proportional to the risk: a negative access test for the cancellation helper, a repeat-request test for idempotency, or a small measured comparison for a performance claim. After a revision, verify that the identified behavior changed. A renamed method does not fix missing authorization. A test that asserts only 'no exception' does not prove that another dealer's appointment stayed unchanged. Review the revised diff and the relevant result.
