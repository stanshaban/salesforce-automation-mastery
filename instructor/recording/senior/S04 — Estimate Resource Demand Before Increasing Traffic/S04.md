## 02 · A limit and an allocation answer different questions

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «A limit and an allocation answer different questions». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

A per-transaction limit bounds one execution. An allocation can constrain an organization over a period or another shared scope. Capacity is the amount of useful work a system can complete under defined conditions. These concepts are related, but they are not interchangeable. An appointment handler can fit comfortably inside its own transaction limits while thousands of invocations consume a shared allowance or overwhelm a downstream service. Northstar expects more dealers to use Anna's booking process. Oleg asks whether ten times as many appointments means ten times as much CPU in one transaction. Usually the first question is how requests are grouped. Ten independent requests differ from one bulk update containing many records, and both differ from a background process that queries additional related data. State the execution shape before doing arithmetic.

## 03 · Calculate a transparent demand model

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Calculate a transparent demand model». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

The fixture assumes one notification request per accepted booking and a separate reconciliation request per group of fifty bookings. These are invented design inputs. At 100 bookings per minute, the model predicts 100 notification requests and two reconciliation requests per minute, before retries. Multiply by the active minutes in the business day rather than automatically assuming round-the-clock traffic. Include a separate allowance for replays, retries, and administrative operations. Now inspect the work inside a save. A handler that issues one query per appointment grows differently from a handler that queries relevant vehicles once for the whole collection. Do not extrapolate CPU as perfectly linear without measuring it: branching, sharing checks, record distribution, and downstream automation can change the relationship. Use the small experiment to identify the shape of the work, then retain uncertainty around its production cost.

## 04 · Queue growth reveals a different failure

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Queue growth reveals a different failure». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Suppose a simulated worker completes 80 notifications per minute while 100 arrive. The backlog grows by approximately twenty per minute under those assumptions. Moving the publisher into Queueable does not remove that difference. It changes when the user waits and which execution limits apply. Queue age, the time the oldest pending item has waited, often describes business impact better than queue length alone. During recovery, capacity must serve new arrivals and reduce old work. A worker that merely matches current arrival rate cannot drain the accumulated backlog. This is a planning equation, not a promise that Salesforce will provide a constant worker rate. Async scheduling is affected by shared resources and platform policies; avoid presenting it as an exact start-time guarantee.

## 05 · Turn the estimate into a verification plan

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Turn the estimate into a verification plan». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Use a small set of synthetic records to compare bulk and single-record execution. Record relevant counters before and after a bounded operation, preserve the same business assertions, and note other automation that ran. A tiny org cannot validate an enterprise rate forecast, but it can reveal an obvious query-per-record pattern. Produce a capacity sheet with assumptions, derived demand, current documented constraints, and unknowns. Highlight the constraint that could be reached first. Recommend a controlled test in an authorized representative environment, including a stop condition for errors or queue growth. The useful output is an honest decision boundary, not a large spreadsheet full of unverified precision.
