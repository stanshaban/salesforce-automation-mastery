## 02 · A product solves a category of work

Salesforce is both a company name and the name people use for its family of business software. A company can use Salesforce products to support work such as sales or customer service. A product provides a collection of capabilities for a particular purpose. This does not mean every Salesforce customer owns every product or sees the same features. Northstar's salespeople need customer details and follow-up work. Its support staff need to track customer problems. A ready-made product can provide a starting point. Northstar may still need changes to match how it arranges test drives. A developer should first understand the available capabilities instead of assuming that every requirement needs a new program.

## 03 · The platform and an individual environment

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «The platform and an individual environment». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

The Salesforce platform provides tools for storing data, controlling access, creating applications, and adding business behaviour. An application brings together screens and functions used for a task. Some changes can be made using configuration: settings chosen through the interface. Others require code: written instructions that a programming language can execute. Both approaches still need clear requirements and testing. An org, short for organization, is a separate Salesforce environment containing its own data, users, and configuration. A user is an account through which a person signs in. Northstar's working org is distinct from your personal learning org. Creating a learning org does not grant access to an employer's customer information.

Anna and Max use the system to carry out sales work. Oleg defines the team's needs and checks whether the result helps the business. A Salesforce administrator changes configuration, manages access, and maintains the application. A Salesforce developer writes and maintains code when the solution needs it. These are responsibilities, not rigid boundaries: a developer also uses configuration, and a small team may combine several jobs.
