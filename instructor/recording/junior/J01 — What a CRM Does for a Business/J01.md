## 02 · Start with a conversation

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Start with a conversation». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Northstar Motors is our fictional car dealership: a business that helps people choose and buy cars. Irina is interested in a vehicle and asks whether she can try driving it before deciding. That short trial is a test drive. Anna is a salesperson. Her job is to answer Irina's questions, find a suitable vehicle, and arrange the next step. A customer is the person receiving help from the business; in this lesson that includes someone who has not bought anything yet. Anna learns that Irina wants a blue car and can speak tomorrow afternoon. She writes a telephone number on a piece of paper. The next day Anna is away. Max, another salesperson, takes over. He has the number but does not know what Anna promised. Calling immediately could mean repeating questions, offering the wrong vehicle, or missing an agreed appointment. This is an information problem before it is a software problem.

## 03 · Separate history, responsibility, and the next action

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Separate history, responsibility, and the next action». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

CRM stands for customer relationship management. A CRM system is software that helps a business keep useful information about customers and its work with them. The useful information is more than a directory of names. Northstar needs the request, previous conversations, agreements, and the work still to be done. Salesforce provides CRM products; we will introduce it separately in J02. History describes something that already happened: Anna discussed the blue vehicle on Monday. A next action describes work that has not happened: call Irina on Tuesday afternoon. Responsibility names the person who should do it: Max. A due time makes the promise concrete. These ideas are related, but one does not replace another. Writing 'customer called' does not tell Max what to do next.

## 04 · Work through the handover

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Work through the handover». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Make a small handover note before opening any software. Use fictional details only. Do not invent a confirmed appointment: Irina has asked about a test drive, but a time has not yet been agreed. Distinguishing an enquiry from a commitment prevents a persuasive-looking screen from hiding an inaccurate business record.
