An interface is the collection of pages, menus, fields, and controls through which a person uses software. Lightning Experience is Salesforce's main modern desktop interface. Your learning org may show different applications or labels from an illustration because its language, configuration, and permissions can differ. We will explain the purpose of each area rather than rely on a pixel-perfect screenshot. The App Launcher, commonly shown as a grid of dots, helps you find available applications and items. Search for Sales to open a sales workspace if it is available. The application name helps you recognize where you are. Its navigation items lead to areas such as Contacts. A contact is information about a person; we will examine the underlying data model in J05 and J07.

## 03 · Move from several records to one record

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Move from several records to one record». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

A record is information about one specific item, such as one person or one test drive. A list shows several records so that you can find and compare them. A list view is a selected way of displaying that list, often with conditions that determine which records appear. 'Recently Viewed' is therefore not a promise to display every contact in the org. Suppose Anna wants Irina's telephone number. She opens Contacts, finds the appropriate person, and opens the record. The record page presents information about that one contact. A Details area commonly displays field values, while Related presents associated information where the page supports it. The list and the record page are different views of the same stored information, not separate copies of the person.

Setup is the configuration area of Salesforce. When permitted, you can reach it through the gear menu. It is where an administrator manages features such as users and object definitions. Entering a telephone number for one customer is everyday data work. Adding a new kind of field to store telephone numbers is a configuration change that can affect many records.
