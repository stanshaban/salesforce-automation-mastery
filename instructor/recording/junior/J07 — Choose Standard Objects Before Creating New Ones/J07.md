## 02 · Match an object to the question it answers

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Match an object to the question it answers». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

A standard object is supplied by Salesforce for a common category of work. A custom object is a structure a team adds for its own application. Starting with standard objects can preserve useful existing behaviour and familiar terminology. It is not a command to force every business concept into an unsuitable object. A Lead commonly represents an early prospect whose suitability or next step is still being assessed. An Account commonly represents a business or organization, and a Contact represents a person associated with business relationships. An Opportunity tracks a potential sale, including its progress. A Case tracks an issue or service request that needs resolution. A customer can therefore be associated with several related records that describe different work, rather than being copied into unrelated 'Customer One' and 'Customer Two' objects.

## 03 · Follow Irina without forcing a conversion story

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Follow Irina without forcing a conversion story». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Irina asks about a City EV. Northstar might initially capture that enquiry as a Lead if its sales process qualifies enquiries that way. When it identifies a real sales opportunity, the team may maintain a Contact for Irina and an Opportunity for the possible purchase. A Lead is not required before every Opportunity: a salesperson can create an Opportunity for an existing relationship. Lead conversion is one supported process, not the definition of all selling. After purchase, Irina reports a problem with a charging cable. That issue is a Case, not a new Opportunity merely because it concerns the same person. If she later considers another vehicle, a separate Opportunity can describe that new potential sale. The records represent different events and responsibilities even though they concern one customer.
