Anna is absent tomorrow and asks Max to help with one appointment. If the need is temporary and specific, manual sharing may fit better than granting access to every sales record. Manual sharing allows an owner or another sufficiently authorized user to grant supported access to an individual record. The share remains one part of the permission picture. Max still needs the required object and field permissions. Read Only sharing does not authorize editing, and Read/Write sharing does not replace object Edit when it is missing. Reuse the layered model from J11 rather than assuming a Share action bypasses the rest of the access design.

## 03 · Distinguish a share from ownership

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Distinguish a share from ownership». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Ownership identifies the responsible owner of a record. Sharing can add access for another recipient without changing that owner. Anna can remain the owner while Max receives access. Transferring ownership changes a different relationship and may alter other access paths as well. Do not memorize the outdated absolute statement that manual shares always disappear after transfer. Current Salesforce documentation describes an org-level setting named Keep manual shares when transferring records. The default behaviour removes manual shares when ownership changes; enabling the setting changes that behaviour. Inspect and record the actual setting before predicting your result.
