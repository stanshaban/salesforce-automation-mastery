## 02 · Define the personas before configuring them

A persona is a description of a type of user and the work that user needs to perform. It is not automatically a Salesforce profile, role, or permission set. For this capstone, Anna represents sales work, Max represents service preparation for the exercise, and Oleg represents management. Max is normally another salesperson in the story; assigning him a temporary service-review responsibility here is an explicit test scenario. Anna should create and manage the appointments she owns. The service reviewer should read Confirmed appointments needed for preparation, without changing appointment data. Oleg should read the appointments covered by the agreed management hierarchy. Do not assume Oleg should edit every record merely because he is a manager. Write the requirement and configure only what it needs.

## 03 · Create an executable matrix

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Create an executable matrix». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Make a matrix whose rows are actions and whose columns are personas. Use concrete records: Anna's Requested appointment, Anna's Confirmed appointment, and an unrelated peer appointment where suitable users exist. Include read, edit, and delete actions, plus at least one field-level restriction. Mark the expected result and the reason for it. For example, the service reviewer may read Anna's Confirmed appointment through the focused sharing rule but should not edit its scheduled time because object Edit is absent. Anna may edit her own appointment but should not gain broad vehicle deletion. Oleg's visibility must be explained through the actual role positions and custom-object hierarchy setting, not through an administrator profile.
