## 02 · A short request contains unanswered decisions

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «A short request contains unanswered decisions». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Oleg asks, 'Please add test-drive booking to Salesforce.' This expresses a useful direction, but it is not yet a complete task. Who makes the booking? What information is required? What does booking mean: recording a request, reserving a vehicle, or confirming a customer appointment? These choices affect the data, permissions, and behaviour that the team will eventually build. Start with the people. Anna needs to record the vehicle and time agreed with Irina so that Max can host the appointment when Anna is away. Anna is the user of this proposed function; Irina is the customer receiving the service. The request does not imply that Irina needs a Salesforce login. Separating the person using the screen from the person affected by the business outcome prevents an unnecessary external portal.

## 03 · Ask questions in a workable order

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Ask questions in a workable order». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

First identify the information. Does the vehicle already exist in the catalogue? How is the customer identified? Which time zone does the team use? Then identify the actions: can Anna create, change, confirm, and cancel the appointment? Finally investigate exceptional situations: a vehicle becomes unavailable, a time is already occupied, or Irina changes her mind. Do not quietly invent answers. Write unknowns as questions and proposals as proposals. Oleg might decide that the first version simply records an appointment after a salesperson checks availability manually. Automatic calendar coordination would then be a later feature. That is a legitimate small scope when it is agreed and described honestly.

## 04 · Define evidence of success and failure

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Define evidence of success and failure». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Acceptance criteria are conditions used to decide whether the agreed behaviour has been delivered. 'Make booking easy' is too ambiguous to test on its own. 'Anna selects an existing vehicle and future time, saves the appointment, reopens it, and sees the same values' describes observable actions and results. A reviewer can repeat that scenario.
