Understand the company's task before configuring an agent. A CRM stores information about customers and the work a company performs with them. Northstar Motors sells cars. Anna and Max are salespeople: they receive a customer's request, identify a vehicle, agree on a time, and record the appointment. Oleg supervises the team. Irina is a fictional customer represented by a Contact record. A support employee can receive a Case when a question needs human attention. Agentforce adds a conversational interface to these concrete operations. In this lesson, a salesperson already signed in to Salesforce uses the agent; the customer does not receive public CRM access.
