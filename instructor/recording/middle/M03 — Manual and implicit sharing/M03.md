## 02 · Understand the mechanism

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Understand the mechanism». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Manual sharing is a deliberate record-level grant to another user or group. It can solve a small exception such as Anna asking Max to help with one appointment. It does not grant object Read or field permissions. An owner may give a record-level permission that Max cannot effectively use until the other security layers allow the operation. Implicit sharing describes access Salesforce derives from particular supported relationships and ownership rules, especially around Account and related standard records. It is platform-managed behavior, not a generic consequence of any lookup. The exact result depends on the objects and sharing configuration. Explain the documented relationship involved rather than claiming that every parent automatically shares all children.
