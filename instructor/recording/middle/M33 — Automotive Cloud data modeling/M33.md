## 02 · Understand the mechanism

The core SAM project uses Vehicle__c to teach Salesforce development with a small owned schema. That custom object is not the Automotive Cloud Vehicle standard object, and deploying the starter does not install the industry product. Similar labels can conceal different APIs, relationships, licenses, and business capabilities. Keep the two models visibly separate. Automotive Cloud's Vehicle Definition extends product information with model-level vehicle characteristics, such as dimensions or powertrain specifications. An individual Vehicle represents a particular vehicle instance. A model definition can therefore describe many actual vehicles. Storing all model specifications repeatedly on every custom vehicle may work for a prototype but creates consistency and maintenance concerns as the business grows.

Irina considers a City EV model, then buys a particular vehicle with its own VIN. The model's battery specification belongs to a shared definition; the individual vehicle's identity and lifecycle belong to the instance. Later, a different person may own or use that same vehicle, while service and warranty history must remain understandable. These are richer relationships than a single Customer__c lookup on a test-drive request. Create a mapping sheet with three columns: core teaching concept, Automotive concept, and unresolved question. Map Vehicle__c.Model__c toward model/definition information, Vehicle__c.VIN__c toward individual vehicle identity, and Contact toward a participant in a business relationship.
