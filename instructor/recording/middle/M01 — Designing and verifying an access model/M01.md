## 02 · Understand the mechanism

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Understand the mechanism». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Northstar Motors has two salespeople, Anna and Max, and a manager, Oleg. Irina is their customer, represented by a Contact record; she is not automatically a Salesforce user. Saying that service staff should see customers leaves several questions unanswered. Which customers, which fields, through which interface, and what may staff change? An access model answers these questions before a profile or sharing rule is edited. Separate four decisions. Object permission determines whether an operation such as reading Test Drive is available at all. Field-level security determines which fields the person may read or edit. Record access determines which individual test drives the person can reach. A business rule can further decide whether an otherwise editable appointment may be cancelled. A person passing one layer has not automatically passed the others.
