## 02 · Understand the mechanism

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Understand the mechanism». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

A sharing mechanism opens access to records. A restriction rule narrows the records a matching user can access through supported paths. Think of two stages: first determine the records available from the ordinary security model, then apply the rule's record criteria where the feature is supported. A restriction rule does not give a user object Read or open a private appointment. Rules have user criteria and record criteria. User criteria identify the people to whom the restriction applies; record criteria identify the records that remain visible to those people. Both must be stated explicitly. A rule that accidentally matches Oleg as well as Anna can hide operational work from the manager, even when the sharing design itself is correct.
