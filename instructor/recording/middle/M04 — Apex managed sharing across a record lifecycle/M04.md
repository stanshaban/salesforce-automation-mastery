## 02 · Understand the mechanism

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Understand the mechanism». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Some access requirements follow business data rather than the role hierarchy. Northstar appoints a manager to an internal project, and that manager needs Read while assigned. A sharing rule based only on fixed groups may not express this relationship. Apex managed sharing lets application logic maintain the specific record-level grant. Create Project__c as a separate custom object with a Text Name, private organization-wide defaults, and Manager__c as a lookup to User. These fields are not in the starter. Add an Apex Sharing Reason named Assigned_Manager. Its API value becomes Assigned_Manager__c in the share record's RowCause. RowCause is the reason for the grant; it lets your process distinguish its work from Manual, Owner, or another application's reason.
