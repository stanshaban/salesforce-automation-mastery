## 02 · Understand the mechanism

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Understand the mechanism». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Change Data Capture describes supported changes to Salesforce records. A custom Platform Event describes a business message chosen by your application. If a downstream system needs a replica of selected record changes, CDC may fit. If it needs the explicit fact that a test drive confirmation was accepted under a particular rule, a custom business event can provide a clearer contract than interpreting every field update. Replay lets a subscriber resume from a previously recorded position within the available event retention window. The replay ID is a stream position token, not a VIN, request ID, or universal permanent event identity. Do not generate it, increment it, or assume it is a consecutive sequence. Store the value supplied by the platform using the subscription mechanism's documented format.
