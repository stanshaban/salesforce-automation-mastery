## 02 · Understand the mechanism

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Understand the mechanism». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

A Case records a customer's service issue. A queue groups work that a team can handle. An assignment rule can place a Case with an owner or queue, but that is not the same as deciding which available employee should receive the next item. Omni-Channel adds routing behavior based on configured eligibility, workload, priority, and, when used, skills. Presence describes whether a person is available for the relevant work. Capacity describes how much work the configuration allows them to handle. A work item consumes capacity according to the chosen model. Queue membership alone does not prove that a person is currently eligible: they may be offline, at capacity, missing a needed skill, or unavailable for the relevant service channel.

## 03 · Northstar Motors worked example

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Northstar Motors worked example». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Use this simplified capacity model: each employee has capacity 4 and each Case costs 2 units. Anna is online with 2 units used; Max is online with all 4 used; Oleg is offline with 0 used. All three belong to the support queue. Only Anna is eligible for the next item. After accepting it, she reaches 4 and a second item must wait unless someone else's eligibility changes. Now Max completes work and drops to 2 used units. He becomes eligible under the simple queue scenario. Add a required EV-service skill that only Anna has: Max's free capacity is no longer sufficient to qualify him. These examples intentionally avoid a tie between equally eligible employees, so they do not invent the product's tie-breaking algorithm.
