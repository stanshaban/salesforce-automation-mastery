## 02 · Understand the mechanism

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Understand the mechanism». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

A useful unit test states a rule in executable form. It prepares known inputs, performs one operation, and checks a meaningful observable result. Code coverage tells you which lines ran; it does not tell you whether a completed appointment was accidentally cancelled or whether the returned value was correct. The strongest test fails when the behavior it protects is broken. Northstar's local rule cancels Requested or Confirmed appointments strictly earlier than a supplied cutoff. Completed, Cancelled, and future appointments remain unchanged. Supplying the cutoff makes the rule deterministic; a test does not depend on the wall clock reaching midnight or on a record that happens to exist in the org. The boundary at exactly the cutoff deserves its own assertion.
