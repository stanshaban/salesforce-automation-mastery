## 02 · Understand the mechanism

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Understand the mechanism». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

A Platform Event is a message on Salesforce's event infrastructure. A producer publishes it, and subscribers react independently. 'Test drive confirmed' describes a business fact; 'confirm this test drive' requests an action. Naming the intent matters because a subscriber should know whether it can trust the referenced change as committed or is being asked to perform it. Publish After Commit ties publication to successful completion of the producing transaction. This is a natural choice for a message announcing a saved confirmation. Publish Immediately decouples publication from the transaction's final outcome, so a subscriber may observe the event before related data commits or even when the producer later rolls back. That behavior can be useful for specific diagnostics but is not interchangeable with a committed business fact.
