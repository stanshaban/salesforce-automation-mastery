## 02 · Choose a narrow practice goal

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Choose a narrow practice goal». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

A useful mock interview has a scope. “Ask me Salesforce questions” can produce a random mixture of definitions, advanced features and outdated assumptions. Choose a level, two topics and a time limit. For example, a Junior session could cover objects, relationships and simple Apex tests. A Middle session could cover a callout failure and LWC state. A Senior session could examine access changes during an integration redesign. Tell the assistant which parts of the Northstar project you have completed. Ask it to question your decisions rather than inventing employment history for you. It should ask one question at a time, wait for your answer and use a follow-up when your explanation is vague. Keep the first session short enough that you can review it properly.

## 03 · Set rules for evidence and feedback

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Set rules for evidence and feedback». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

An AI interviewer can be mistaken, especially about changing platform behaviour and product availability. Ask it to separate factual feedback from communication feedback. A factual correction should include a current official source that you can verify. “Your answer was difficult to follow” is communication feedback and needs an example from your response rather than a documentation link. Use a small rubric: technical correctness, reasoning, evidence and clarity. Score each as missing, partial or clear. If you say “use asynchronous Apex,” the follow-up should ask which mechanism, why it fits and what happens on failure. This tests understanding more effectively than giving full credit for a keyword.

## 04 · Answer with a decision and a reason

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Answer with a decision and a reason». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Use a simple response structure: state the relevant fact or decision, explain why, give an example and mention an important boundary. For “Why use a lookup here?”, you might explain that the test-drive record has its own ownership and sharing requirements, show the model and discuss what a master-detail alternative would change. Do not force every answer into a long speech; the structure is a guide, not a script. When you do not know, identify the missing fact and explain how you would verify it. “I would check the current limit and the expected volume before choosing the batch size” is useful if you can also explain the design issue. Avoid using uncertainty as a way to say nothing: give the part you understand, then mark the boundary.

## 05 · Review the transcript and retry one problem

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Review the transcript and retry one problem». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

After the session, choose two weak answers and verify the assistant’s feedback against official documentation or your own reproducible test. Keep a table with question, your original answer, confirmed issue and next exercise. Reject a correction if its evidence does not support it; the assistant is a practice partner, not the final authority. Repeat a related question with a changed condition. If the first question used a single record, ask about a batch. If it assumed private sharing, ask what changes with another access path. You should be able to explain the changed reasoning. Repeating the exact wording until it sounds fluent is not enough to establish understanding.
