## 02 · Start with a specific piece of work

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Start with a specific piece of work». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

An experience statement is useful when another person can understand your responsibility. “Worked with Salesforce” names a platform but says little about your work. “Built a test-drive request form that validates the vehicle and records the customer’s chosen time” describes a feature someone can discuss with you. Use three kinds of evidence carefully. Commercial work comes from a real employment or client context. A personal project is work you designed and completed yourself. A guided exercise follows a learning path. All three can demonstrate knowledge, but they are different contexts. Label the Northstar project as a training project unless you have separately delivered it for a real business.

## 03 · Build a short explanation from five parts

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Build a short explanation from five parts». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Write the situation, the requirement, your contribution, the result and how you checked it. For Northstar, the situation is that salespeople need a consistent way to request test drives. The requirement is to capture a customer, an available vehicle and a time. Your contribution might be the validation logic and its tests. The result is an implemented feature. Your evidence is a repeatable test and a short demonstration. Be exact about ownership. If another learner designed the data model and you wrote the LWC, say that. If you extended a supplied starter class, identify the extension. This does not weaken the account; it shows that you understand the boundary of your responsibility. Avoid describing every part of a team project as something you personally delivered.

## 04 · Use measurements only when you have them

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Use measurements only when you have them». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

A result does not have to contain a percentage. “Handles a test batch of 200 records without queries inside the loop” is a concrete technical result if you ran that test. “Improved sales productivity by 40%” is not supported merely because your form takes fewer clicks in a demonstration. Business impact needs a suitable baseline, a measurement period and a way to connect the change to the observation. If you have a measurement, keep its scope. A local test that falls from 800 milliseconds to 300 milliseconds does not prove that production response times improved by the same amount. Explain the environment, the sample and the limitation. If you did not measure an outcome, describe what the feature enables and how you verified its behaviour instead of inventing a metric.

## 05 · Prepare a short and a detailed version

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Prepare a short and a detailed version». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Write a three-sentence version for a CV or introduction, then a two-minute version for an interview. The short version should name the feature, your work and the evidence. The longer version should add one difficulty, one decision and one limitation. Practice both without reading the text word for word. For example: “I built a Salesforce training application for test-drive requests. I implemented bulk-safe validation and tests for unavailable vehicles and mixed valid and invalid records. I can demonstrate the behaviour in a development org; I have not deployed it for a real dealership.” That explanation gives the interviewer useful questions to ask without creating a false impression of production experience.
