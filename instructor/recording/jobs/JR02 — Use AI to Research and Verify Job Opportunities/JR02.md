## 02 · Give the assistant a bounded research task

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Give the assistant a bounded research task». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

An AI assistant can help turn a broad search into structured work. It can suggest search terms, compare descriptions and extract responsibilities. Whether it can find current vacancies depends on the tools available in that session. A model that cannot access current pages must not be treated as a live job feed. Start with the target statement from JR01. Add the locations or working arrangements you are willing to consider, the type of role and any explicit exclusions. Keep sensitive personal details out of the research prompt. The assistant does not need your full address, passport or private employment documents to help compare public descriptions. Ask it to distinguish observed facts from questions that remain unanswered.

## 03 · Search with evidence fields

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Search with evidence fields». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Ask for a small batch rather than hundreds of results. For each candidate, request the employer, exact role title, direct source URL, visible location, stated remote conditions, date checked and the specific responsibility that matches your target. If the page does not state a condition, the table should say “Not stated.” Suppose a result says “Remote Salesforce Developer — Europe.” Before treating that as a match, open the employer page. The actual description may restrict hiring to a short list of countries or require regular office visits. The assistant’s summary is useful only when it preserves those limits. A search result snippet or an aggregator’s copied title is not enough to settle eligibility.

## 04 · Check the original page and remove duplicates

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Check the original page and remove duplicates». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Use a verification pass with four questions. Does the page exist? Does it still present an open application route? Does the description match the summary? Does the employer identity match the domain and application destination? A page can remain visible after a role closes, so a URL alone is not proof that applications are still accepted. Duplicates often appear under several agencies, job boards or slightly different titles. Compare employer, location, responsibilities and any job reference number. Keep one primary record and add secondary links only if they help. Do not merge two different roles simply because their titles match. Record the reason for a duplicate decision so you can reverse it when new information appears.

## 05 · Turn research into a decision

> 🎬 ЭКРАН. Раздел «Turn research into a decision». Продолжай показ подготовленного примера. Выдели входные данные и решение именно этого раздела. Не прокручивай целый урок вместо демонстрации. Если это аналитическое упражнение, заполняй его рабочую таблицу по одному решению.

Give each verified result one next step: apply, investigate a missing condition, save for later or skip. Write a short reason. “Apply: the role mainly uses Apex and LWC, and I have two relevant examples” is better than an unsupported percentage fit score. “Investigate: remote hiring countries are not stated” preserves uncertainty instead of hiding it. AI can draft a comparison, but you decide whether the factual evidence supports it. Review one result that you rejected as well as one you accepted. This catches overly narrow filters and makes the process useful even when the first search produces few matches. Keep the date of verification; current vacancies can change after the lesson.
